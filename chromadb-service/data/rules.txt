--------------------------
Next are the rules of behavior
--------------------------

About Arrival.
After getting off the plane the passenger is requred to do next things in this particular order.
He can visit other points for entertainment or personal needs but is not required to.

1. Get your luggage if you have it.
2. Pass security checkpoint on exit.
3. Exit the airport.

--------------------------

About Departure.
To board the plane the passenger is requred to do next things in this particular order.
He can visit other points for entertainment or personal needs but is not required to.

1. Pass security checkpoint on entrance.
2. Pass the check-in.
3. Board your plane.
